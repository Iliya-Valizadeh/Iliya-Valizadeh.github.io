About

Who you would be working with

I am a data science student at York University in Toronto, and an international student paying my own way through school. For the past couple of years I have worked through full course loads to cover tuition, and built data projects in the hours in between. That kind of schedule does not leave much room for excuses, so I stopped making them.

I am kind to the people I work with, and I mean that as a promise rather than a line on paper. I share credit. When I do not know something I ask instead of guessing, and I take feedback without getting defensive, because getting it right matters more to me than being right.

When I pick up something new I go deep on it fast. I taught myself the tools behind my projects because I wanted them to work. I also hold a hard line on honesty: I will not put a number on a page that I cannot back up with the code behind it.

If you are building something and you want someone reliable, curious, and good to have in the room, I would like to talk.
